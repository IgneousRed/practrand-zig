# Project direction

Develop a faster, simpler Zig replacement for PractRand statistical testing tools, preserving established behavior except for explicitly documented departures. Numerical compatibility and reproducible expected fixtures remain central.

Maintained usage, architecture, numerical policy and compatibility records are public in [the README](../README.md) and `docs/`. Planning records and historical research are maintained separately at their approved visibility; they are not public build prerequisites or implicit work authorization.
